Our code is available under MIT. That license applies to the program, not to videos, music or other material downloaded with it. External tools and bundled runtime components keep their own licenses; see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Use the app only for material you have the right to save, through access you are entitled to use. Follow the source's terms and applicable law. Public availability, a successful download or an open-source license does not by itself establish permission to copy.

The app does not add DRM, paywall or private-access bypass features. It makes no promise that every site works or that every use is lawful. The MIT warranty disclaimer applies only to the extent permitted by law; mandatory user rights are not waived.

For example, [YouTube's published terms for France](https://www.youtube.com/static?template=terms&gl=FR&hl=en) restrict downloading and automated access, with their stated exceptions. French [CPI L122-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278912/) limits the private-copy exception to lawful sources and private use. These are not a universal authorization for all downloads.

Running an online service, especially one with advertising, has additional legal and privacy questions. This desktop release does not include that service. A disclaimer alone does not eliminate an operator's obligations. Seek advice for your jurisdiction if your intended use is uncertain.

This is a practical use notice, not a legal opinion. Source review date: 6 October 2026.
